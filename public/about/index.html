About Gigi's NY Style Pizza in Long Branch

Gigi's NY Style Pizza & Restaurant is a family-run pizzeria at 140 Brighton Avenue in the West End of Long Branch, New Jersey. The shop is run day to day by its owner-operators, Tommy Basile and Kenneth Gambella, two friends and partners.

See the Menu Call (732) 377-2468

What we make

We make real New York-style pizza: hand-stretched dough, house tomato sauce and real mozzarella, sold as whole pies and by the slice. Alongside the round pies there are Grandma and Sicilian squares, stuffed and specialty pizzas like the hot-honey Bee Sting, The Fonz and the vodka-sauced Drunken Grandma, plus heroes, pasta and Italian dinners.

There is gluten-free and vegan pizza, and breakfast is served all day. It is a full neighborhood kitchen, open year-round, not just a summer slice stop.

Pickup, delivery and parties

Order online at gigislongbranch.com, which goes straight to our own register and kitchen, or call (732) 377-2468. Gigi's runs its own drivers across Long Branch and nearby towns; see delivery areas and fees. For groups there is catering, and kids can make their own pies at a pizza party in the shop.
