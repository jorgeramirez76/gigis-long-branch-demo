What Gigi's Caters

We cater the full Gigi's menu: hand-stretched New York-style pizza, Grandma squares, and specialty pies, plus hot and cold heroes, pasta, salads, appetizers, and full Italian dinners. It travels well and feeds a crowd.

Good to know

Do you cater offices and parties in Long Branch?

Yes. Gigi's caters offices, parties, and gatherings with pizza, Grandma squares, heroes, pasta, salads, and appetizers. Call (732) 377-2468 to plan your order.

How far in advance should I place a catering order?

The more notice you can give, the better, especially for larger groups. Call (732) 377-2468 with your date and headcount and we'll help you plan.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options for catering?

Yes. We make gluten-free NY-style pizza (prepared in a shared kitchen, so not guaranteed allergen-free) and vegan pizza with dairy-free cheese and vegetable toppings. Please tell staff about any allergies.

Do you deliver catering orders?

Yes, we offer pickup and delivery across Long Branch and nearby towns including Pier Village, Elberon, West Long Branch, Monmouth Beach, Deal, Eatontown, Allenhurst, and Oceanport. Call (732) 377-2468 for details.
