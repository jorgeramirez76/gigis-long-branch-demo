Good to know

Do you deliver to my town?

Gigi's delivers to Long Branch, West Long Branch, Monmouth Beach, Oceanport, Eatontown, Tinton Falls, Deal, Allenhurst, Loch Arbour, Oakhurst, Asbury Park and Shrewsbury. If your town isn't listed, call (732) 377-2468 — we may still be able to help.

How much is delivery?

$3 to Long Branch and West Long Branch, and $5 to every other town we cover. It's a flat fee, the same whether you order online or over the phone.

How late do you deliver?

Delivery runs until 10 PM. The kitchen keeps going after that — pickup is available until we close, 11 PM Monday through Wednesday and midnight Thursday through Sunday.

Is there a delivery minimum?

Call (732) 377-2468 and we'll tell you — it isn't set on the website.
