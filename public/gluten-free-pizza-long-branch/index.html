An honest note on our shared kitchen

We want you to order with clear expectations. Our gluten-free pizzas are prepared and baked in the same kitchen as our regular pies, so we can't promise a fully allergen-free or gluten-free-certified pizza.

If you have celiac disease or a serious gluten allergy, please tell our staff when you order. We'll take extra care, and you can decide what's right for you.

Good to know

Is Gigi's gluten-free pizza safe for celiacs?

Gigi's gluten-free pizza is made on a gluten-free crust, but it's prepared and baked in a shared kitchen, so it isn't certified allergen-free and there's a risk of cross-contact. If you have celiac disease, please tell our staff when you order so we can take extra care.

Can I get toppings on the gluten-free crust?

Toppings are charged separately on the gluten-free pie and are not selectable online yet. Call (732) 377-2468 and we'll build it with what you want. If you need it dairy-free as well, call (732) 377-2468 and we'll tell you what's possible that day. Order online or call (732) 377-2468 if you'd like help building your pie.

Does Gigi's deliver gluten-free pizza in Long Branch?

Yes. Gigi's offers pickup and delivery seven days a week. We deliver gluten-free pizza across Long Branch, the West End, Pier Village, Elberon, West Long Branch, Monmouth Beach, Deal, Eatontown, Allenhurst, and Oceanport. Order at gigislongbranch.com or call (732) 377-2468.

How much is a gluten-free pizza at Gigi's?

A gluten-free pie is $17.68, the same as a regular plain pie. Gluten-free pasta is a $2.60 substitution on many pasta dishes, and gluten-free bread is a $2.08 substitution on a breakfast sandwich.

Do you have gluten-free pasta and bread, or only pizza?

All three. Gigi's stocks a gluten-free pizza crust, gluten-free pasta as a swap on many pasta dishes, and gluten-free bread for breakfast sandwiches.

How late can I get gluten-free pizza?

Gluten-free pizza is available for delivery until 10 PM, and for pickup right up to closing — 11 PM Monday through Wednesday, midnight Thursday through Sunday.
