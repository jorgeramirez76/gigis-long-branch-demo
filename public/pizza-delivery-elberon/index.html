NY-Style Pizza, Brought to Your Elberon Door

Elberon is one of Long Branch's calmest, most residential stretches — tree-lined streets and family homes. When it's a stay-in night, Gigi's brings the pizzeria to you.

We're a neighborhood shop on Brighton Avenue in West End, and Elberon is part of our regular delivery area. Same hand-stretched New York-style pies and Grandma squares we make for the folks around the corner.

More Than Pizza — the Whole Menu Delivers

Feeding the family or the office? Beyond our specialty pies, we deliver hot and cold heroes, pasta, fresh salads, appetizers, and full Italian dinners.

We also make a gluten-free NY-style pizza (prepared in a shared kitchen, so not guaranteed allergen-free — please tell us if you're celiac) and a vegan pizza with dairy-free cheese and vegetable toppings.
