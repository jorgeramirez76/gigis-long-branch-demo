Your neighborhood pizzeria in the West End

Gigi's isn't delivering into the West End from across town — we're in it. Our shop sits at 140 Brighton Avenue, so a slice, a whole pie, or a hot hero is just around the corner.

Everything's made the way a New York pizzeria should: thin, hand-stretched, foldable slices, Grandma squares, specialty pies, plus subs, pasta, salads, and full Italian dinners. Open 10 AM to 11 PM Monday through Wednesday, and to midnight Thursday through Sunday.

Pickup or fast local delivery

Walk in and grab your order, or have it brought to you. Because we're right here in the West End, your food doesn't travel far.

We also deliver to Pier Village, Elberon, West Long Branch, Monmouth Beach, Deal, Eatontown, Allenhurst, and Oceanport. Order online at gigislongbranch.com or call (732) 377-2468.

Gluten-free and vegan pizza too

We make NY-style pizza on a gluten-free crust, and a vegan pizza that's its own menu item rather than a regular pie with the cheese left off.

Our gluten-free crust is prepared in a shared kitchen, so it isn't guaranteed allergen-free. If you have celiac disease, please let us know when you order.
