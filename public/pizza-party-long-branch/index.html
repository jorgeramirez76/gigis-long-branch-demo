Good to know

How much is a kids' pizza party?

Packages start at $15 per person. Call (732) 377-2468 and we'll walk you through the options and get your date on the calendar.

What do the kids actually do?

Each child rolls out their own dough, adds tomato sauce, cheese and their favorite toppings, then watches their personal pizza bake. Every child also gets a full pizza-making lesson from our chef.

Can we add chef hats, aprons or t-shirts?

Yes — you can upgrade with personalized chef hats, personalized aprons, an “I'm a Gigi's Pizza Maker” t-shirt, and a pizza maker certificate. Ask when you call to book.

How many kids can come, and how long is the party?

That depends on the day and the size of your group — give us a call at (732) 377-2468 and we'll set it up around your headcount and date.

Is there food for the parents?

Of course — the full Gigi's menu is available: NY-style pies, heroes, pasta, salads and more. Mention it when you book and we'll have it ready.

What about allergies or gluten-free?

We make gluten-free NY-style pizza (prepared in a shared kitchen, so it isn't guaranteed allergen-free) and can work around common requests. Please tell us about any allergies when you book.
